## Scores

### Contextual Moat — 4/5
*Workflow depth × switching cost. Would users leave in a weekend if a competitor showed up?*

**Score rationale:** 
Once residents start using the local digital wallet and verified accounts, it is hard for them to leave. However, this only works if local businesses actually take the time to use their side of the app.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
Nextdoor / WhatsApp Street Groups.

---

### Data Advantage — 4/5
The platform collects highly valuable, unique data on foot traffic, local searches, and neighborhood mood. The catch is that you need a massive amount of users on day one for this data to be worth selling to the council or businesses.

**Score rationale:**

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
Google Maps / Legacy Civic Consultants

---

### Platform Exposure — 3/5
*Encroachment risk × pivot speed. If Apple/Google/OpenAI ships your hero feature native — then what?*

**Score rationale:**
The app relies heavily on outside tech like Apple/Google Wallets, Google Maps, and AI tools. If Apple or Google suddenly change their wallet rules, it could break your business model.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
Apple / Google (Wallet ecosystem policy changes).
